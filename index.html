Spaced Repetition

The One Rule That Makes Everything Work:

Never end a study session without reviewing what you studied at the START of that session. Spend the last 10 minutes re-reading your first 10 minutes of notes. This single habit doubles retention.

  • Make cards for: Drug side effects, clinical features, diagnostic criteria — not whole topics

MRCP PACES — Building Clinical Skills During House Job
Your identified weakness — must be built daily, not last-minute
▾
Cannot be crammed.

PACES is clinical performance under observation. You cannot build systematic examination technique in the final month. You build it during house job at Nishtar, every single patient, starting Day 1.

The House Job PACES Protocol
  • Every patient: take a structured history as if an examiner is watching
  • Every examination: complete and in correct sequence — not casually
  • Every week: present 2 cases formally to a senior and ask for direct feedback
  • ECGs: interpret every ECG yourself before confirming with senior — minimum 5/week
  • CXRs: interpret first, then confirm. Never just accept the report.
  • Build your own examination checklist per system — follow it every single time
